Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Ziad Clips ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Ziad Ahmed that posts videos to the operator's own TikTok account after explicit authorization.

What we collect

  • TikTok basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via TikTok Login Kit, solely to establish the authorized connection.
  • TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload and publish videos the operator selects.

How we use it

Data is used exclusively to publish videos to the operator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. No videos are posted without the operator's explicit per-video confirmation.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not collect analytics, contacts, or content from anyone other than the authorizing account owner.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored encrypted and can be revoked at any time from TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions, which deletes our access immediately.
